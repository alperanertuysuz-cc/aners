Concept missionSolar gravitational lens

Use the Sun as a lens.

About 550 AU out, the Sun’s gravity bends starlight into a focal line. LUMEN/550 is a fleet of sail-driven probes built to reach it, then photograph a planet around another star: continents, coastlines, clouds.

Flight spec · concept targets

The aperture is the Sun. The hard part is the trip.

A lens 1.39 million kilometers wide costs nothing to build. Putting a telescope at its focus takes a sail, two decades and a lot of patience. The figures are design targets for this concept, grounded in published solar-lens mission studies.

Propulsion

≈25AU / yr

A solar sail dives to 0.1 AU, then rides sunlight outward faster than anything yet flown. Voyager 1 manages about 3.6.

Sail · perihelion 0.1 AU

Light gain

≈1011@ 1 µm

At one micron the Sun amplifies a distant planet’s light roughly a hundred billion times. Physics does the heavy lifting; the telescope can stay small.

Gravitational focusing

Optics

1.0m aperture

A modest telescope behind an internal coronagraph blocks the solar disk and keeps only the thin Einstein ring glowing around it.

Telescope · coronagraph

Fleet

20craft

Launched as a string of pearls, one a year. Each samples a different slice of the ring; together they rebuild a single image.

String-of-pearls cadence

Link

3.18days one-way

Light time at 550 AU. Every command is three days stale when it lands, so each craft navigates, points and recovers by itself.

Optical downlink

Resolution

≈13km / pixel

An Earth-size planet at 30 parsecs, reconstructed at about 1,000 × 1,000 pixels after roughly a year of integration.

Flight profile

Four phases · ~22 years to focus

  1. 01

    2035

    Launch

    Rideshare to solar escape with the sail stowed and the coronagraph sealed.

  2. 02

    0.1 AU

    Perihelion dive

    The sail unfurls about 21 solar radii from the Sun: maximum push, maximum heat.

  3. 03

    ~22 yr

    Cruise

    Coast outward at ≈25 AU a year, crossing the heliopause around year five.

  4. 04

    548 AU →

    Focal line

    Coronagraph on. The Einstein ring resolves and imaging continues outward to ~900 AU.
